The Scumbug is a reusable floating sponge that absorbs body oils, lotions and grime at the waterline, where scum lines form. The brand, made by Rola-Chem, claims it absorbs up to 40 times its weight in contaminants.

It is a chemical-free supplement to normal water care, not a replacement for sanitizer, shock or filtration. Owner reviews on Amazon consistently describe fewer scum rings and cleaner filters, especially in inflatable tubs with small cartridges.
