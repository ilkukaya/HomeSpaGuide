Coleman is technically a licensee — the **SaluSpa** range is built on Bestway's hardware platform with a Coleman skin and a US-routed warranty pipeline. That's not a bad thing. You get most of what the underlying Bestway tub offers (jet system, EnergySense cover, ChemConnect dispenser), plus the broader US dealer network of a name-brand outdoor company.

We recommend Coleman to first-time buyers more often than any other inflatable. The Tahiti and Miami models in particular are the closest thing to a "default pick" in this category — neither pretends to be premium nor cuts corners enough to age out in a season. They set up in under an hour, accept generic 4-pleat filter cartridges, and are kind to your electricity meter once water is up to temperature.

The trade-off: Coleman's pricing on identical hardware tends to be ~15% above Bestway's direct retail. If you're shopping on spec and don't need a US-branded badge, Bestway will often be the better number.
