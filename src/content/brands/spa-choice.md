SpaChoice is a small Midwest chemicals company that has quietly been the OEM behind a long list of more famous private-label spa chemistry kits. We started covering them as a value alternative and stayed because the active-ingredient labels read identically to the big-three brands — at meaningfully lower per-gallon cost.

Their **bromine and chlorine tablets** are the products we recommend most often. Both are NSF-certified and ship in re-sealable 1.5-lb tubs rather than the cheaper 6-month bulk pails. Customer reports on Amazon consistently mention predictable dissolution rates at hot-tub temperatures and a long shelf life when the tubs stay sealed.

What to skip: their pH balancers are fine but not differentiated. If you already buy a balancer you trust, there's no reason to switch. SpaChoice earns its place in the sanitizer slot.
