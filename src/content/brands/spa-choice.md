SpaChoice sells a full line of spa and hot tub chemicals on Amazon, typically in single containers and multi-packs. The line includes **brominating tablets** (98% BCDMH), **chlorine granules** (99% sodium dichlor), and single-ingredient balancers such as **pH Increaser** (sodium carbonate) and **pH Decreaser** (sodium bisulfate).

What we like is the plain labeling: active ingredients and dosing directions are listed clearly, which makes SpaChoice products easy to compare against other brands. Many listings note the products are made in the USA.
