Leisure Time makes chemicals specifically for spas and hot tubs rather than pools. Its range covers sanitizers, oxidizers, balancers and specialty products, and many of them are widely stocked on Amazon in single bottles and multi-packs.

The products we cover most often are **Renew**, a pH-buffered non-chlorine shock labeled for chlorine, bromine and mineral systems; **Foam Down**, a concentrated defoamer; and **Filter Clean**, an overnight-soak cartridge cleaner. Each carries clear label directions, which makes them easy starting points for new hot tub owners.
