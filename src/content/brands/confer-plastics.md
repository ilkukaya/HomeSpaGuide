Confer Plastics is a US manufacturer of blow-molded plastic products for pools and spas. Its hot tub range runs from compact steps like the **Handi-Step** to full spa stairs with handrails and storage steps.

The Handi-Step is the model we cover most: a two-tread resin step rated for 300 lb with a reversible tread that fits round or straight-sided tubs.
