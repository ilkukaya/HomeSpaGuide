Lifesmart is best known in this category for **plug-and-play hard-shell spas** — tubs with a rotationally molded polyethylene shell, foam insulation and real water jets that plug into a standard 110V outlet. That combination targets buyers who have outgrown an inflatable but don't want to pay an electrician for a 240V circuit.

The range spans compact 4-person tubs with 13 jets up to 5-person models with a lounger, ozonator and waterfall. Across owner reviews on Amazon and in r/hottubs, the recurring praise is for easy installation and a tough, low-maintenance shell; the recurring complaint is the one shared by every 110V tub — slower heating and gentler jets than a 240V spa.
