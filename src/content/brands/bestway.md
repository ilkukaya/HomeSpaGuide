Bestway sells more inflatable spas globally than any other brand, mostly through the **Lay-Z-Spa** (Europe) and **SaluSpa-by-Bestway** (US) lines. They've leaned harder into engineering than Coleman or Intex — the HydroJet Pro models in particular are the first inflatables to feel meaningfully closer to a hard-shell experience.

What we like: the **freeze shield** feature on newer Lay-Z-Spas runs the pump intermittently below 40°F to stop water lines from cracking. It's the single most useful inflatable-spa innovation of the last five years and isn't something Intex or Coleman match. Hydrojet pressure is also genuinely strong — not "marketing strong" — though you'll want to switch back to AirJet mode for daily 30-minute soaks if you care about your electricity bill.

What we'd flag: covers and inflatable rings are slightly more delicate than Intex's. The DuraPlus laminate handles chlorine fine but is more sensitive to UV — if your tub lives in full sun all summer, plan on the cover being a wear item every 2–3 years.
