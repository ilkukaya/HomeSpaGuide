Pleatco is a long-established name in replacement filter cartridges. Its spa cartridges are sold by Pleatco part number (for example, **PRB50-IN**) and cross-referenced to equivalent Unicel and Filbur numbers and to the OEM part numbers used by spa manufacturers.

The practical advice for any Pleatco purchase: match the part number and dimensions on your old cartridge rather than shopping by tub brand. Hard-shell spas often use several cartridge sizes across model years.
