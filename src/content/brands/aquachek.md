AquaChek is one of the most widely recognized test-strip brands in the pool and spa aisle. Its lineup includes multi-parameter pool strips and spa-specific products such as the **SPA 6-in-1**, which reads total bromine, total chlorine, free chlorine, pH, total alkalinity and total hardness on one strip.

Test strips trade some precision for speed compared with liquid drop kits. For most home spa owners that trade is worth it, because a quick dip before each soak is a routine people actually keep.
