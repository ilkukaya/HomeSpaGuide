Poolmaster's catalog spans maintenance tools and recreational accessories for pools and spas. Its **Essential Collection** covers basic, everyday items such as the 18307 floating thermometer, which reads up to 120°F and suits hot tub temperatures.

For hot tub owners, Poolmaster is a budget-friendly source of simple accessories rather than chemicals or equipment.
