faqs:
  - question: Are inflatable hot tubs worth it?
    answer: For most first-time buyers, yes. They cost a fraction of hard-shell tubs, set up in under an hour, and are surprisingly durable when cared for. The trade-off is shorter lifespan (3–6 seasons typical) and weaker insulation.
  - question: How much electricity does a hot tub use?
    answer: A standard 110V inflatable tub uses about 5–8 kWh/day in summer and 8–14 kWh/day in winter. Hard-shell tubs with better insulation often use less in cold weather despite higher wattage.
