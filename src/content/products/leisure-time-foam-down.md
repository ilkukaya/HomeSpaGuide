A foamy hot tub is a common complaint, and Leisure Time Foam Down is the quick fix many owners keep under the steps for exactly that moment.

## What the manufacturer says

Leisure Time describes Foam Down as a concentrated, non-oily foam suppressant compatible with chlorine, bromine, ozone and biguanide. Directions: shake, add about 1/2 oz directly onto the foam, repeat as needed. The 32 fl oz bottle has a flip-top cap.

## What owners praise

- Foam disappears within moments of application
- A little goes a long way
- Easy one-handed pour from the flip-top
- Works regardless of sanitizer

## What owners complain about

- Foam returns if the underlying cause is not addressed
- Heavy use can leave residue at the waterline

## Who should buy it

If your priority is a fast fix before a soak, this is the cleanest fit. If foam shows up every time you use the tub, put effort into prevention first: a weekly [non-chlorine shock](/products/leisure-time-renew-non-chlorine-shock), a [Scumbug](/products/scumbug-scum-absorber-2-pack) to soak up oils, and rinsing swimsuits without detergent.
