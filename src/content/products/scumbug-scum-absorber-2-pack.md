The Scumbug is the simplest upgrade most hot tubs can get: toss it in, and it quietly soaks up the body oils and lotions that cause scum lines and foam.

## What the manufacturer says

Made by Rola-Chem, the Scumbug is a floating oil-absorbing sponge for spas, hot tubs and pools. The brand claims it absorbs up to 40 times its weight in oils and contaminants without clogging filters, and that it is reusable after a rinse and squeeze. This listing is a 2-pack.

## What owners praise

- Fewer scum rings at the waterline
- Filters stay cleaner for longer, especially small inflatable cartridges
- Nothing to measure, mix or install
- A 2-pack lets you rotate one while the other dries

## What owners complain about

- Sponges wear out and need replacing eventually
- Some owners expected it to fix cloudy water, which is a chemistry problem

## Who should buy it

If you own an inflatable spa with a small cartridge, this is the cleanest fit, because it catches oils before they coat the filter pleats. Hard-shell owners also benefit, though bigger filters make the effect less noticeable.
