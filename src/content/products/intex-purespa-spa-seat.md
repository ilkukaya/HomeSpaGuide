Inflatable hot tubs have flat floors, which leaves shorter bathers and kids sitting low in the water. The Intex 28502E is the factory booster seat for that problem.

## What the manufacturer says

Intex lists the 28502E as a removable, contoured seat for PureSpa tubs with two height settings, about 3" or 5.8" of lift, and a slip-resistant laminated surface. Dimensions are about 18.5" L x 14" W x 8-5/8" H.

## What owners praise

- Shorter adults finally sit with shoulders out of the water
- Kids can soak comfortably without standing
- Stays put once set up correctly
- Matches the look of the PureSpa

## What owners complain about

- Getting trapped air out during setup takes a little patience
- In 2- and 4-person tubs, a seat noticeably reduces legroom

## Who should buy it

If anyone in your household is under about average height, this is the cleanest fit for a PureSpa. If your concern is getting in and out of the tub, [spa steps](/products/confer-plastics-handi-step) solve that part instead.
