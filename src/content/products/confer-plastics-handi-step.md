Climbing over a hot tub wall with wet feet is awkward, and the Confer Plastics Handi-Step is one of the most common low-cost fixes.

## What the manufacturer says

Confer rates the Handi-Step for 300 lb and describes a reversible tread that fits against round or straight-sided spas. Published dimensions are about 28.5" W x 23" D x 14" H. It snaps together without hardware and is made from UV-resistant blow-molded plastic.

## What owners praise

- Solid and stable under foot
- Fits both round inflatables and square hard-shell tubs
- Assembly takes minutes
- Holds up outdoors across seasons, per many owner reviews

## What owners complain about

- No handrail
- Wet treads can get slick, especially with leaves or grass on them

## Who should buy it

If your priority is an easy, stable step into an inflatable or a low-walled hard-shell tub, this is the cleanest fit. If someone in the household needs something to hold onto, choose steps with handrails.
