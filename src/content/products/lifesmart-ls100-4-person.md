The Lifesmart LS100 is for buyers who have outgrown an inflatable, or want a hard-shell tub, but don't want to pay for 240V wiring.

## Headline specs

Per Lifesmart's published spec, the LS100 measures 70" x 61" x 32", holds about 200 gallons, weighs roughly 265 lbs dry and seats four in molded bucket seats. It runs 13 water jets from a 1.0 HP single-speed pump, plugs into a standard 110V/15A outlet and includes full foam insulation, a multi-color LED light and a thermal locking cover. The shell is rotationally molded polyethylene rather than acrylic.

## What owners praise

- **No electrician.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently cite the 110V plug as the deciding factor.
- **Real jets.** Buyers moving from inflatables describe the water-jet massage as a clear upgrade over bubbles.
- **Durable shell.** The rotomolded body is regularly described as tough and low-maintenance.
- **Heat retention** versus inflatables, thanks to the foam insulation and rigid cover.

## What owners complain about

- **Slow heat-up and recovery** — a trait of every 110V tub.
- **Jet strength** is modest compared with 240V acrylic spas.
- **Tight fit** for four large adults.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

If your priority is year-round use without new wiring, this is the cleanest fit because it adds insulation and a rigid shell while keeping inflatable-style installation. If you want a lounger and more jets, the [Lifesmart 5-Person 28-Jet](/products/lifesmart-5-person-28-jet) is the step up. If budget is the main constraint, an inflatable like the [Bestway SaluSpa Santorini HydroJet](/products/bestway-saluspa-santorini-hydrojet) offers water jets for less, but with a shorter expected lifespan.

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, the Lifesmart 5-Person holds 300 gallons and has 28 jets versus 200 gallons and 13 jets here.
