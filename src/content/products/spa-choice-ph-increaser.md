Every hot tub owner eventually needs to nudge pH back up, and SpaChoice's 1-lb pH Increaser is a simple, single-ingredient way to do it.

## What the manufacturer says

SpaChoice lists the product as 100% sodium carbonate, to be used when pH is below 7.2, with target ranges of pH 7.2–7.8 and total alkalinity 80–140 ppm. It is labeled compatible with chlorine, bromine and biguanide systems and made in the USA.

## What owners praise

- Straightforward single-ingredient formula
- A 1-lb container that suits small inflatable tubs without wasting product
- Dissolves readily when pre-mixed or broadcast with jets running
- Works regardless of sanitizer choice

## What owners complain about

- It lifts alkalinity along with pH, so heavy-handed dosing can overshoot
- No dosing scoop in the container, according to a number of reviews

## Who should buy it

If your water tests low on pH after fills or sanitizer doses, this is the cleanest fit. If your pH runs high instead (common with fresh fills in some regions), start with the [SpaChoice pH Decreaser](/products/spa-choice-ph-decreaser).
