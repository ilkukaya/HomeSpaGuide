High pH is one of the most common hot tub water problems, and SpaChoice's pH Decreaser is a simple dry-acid way to bring it back into range.

## What the manufacturer says

SpaChoice lists the product as 100% sodium bisulfate, to be used when pH is above 7.8, with target ranges of pH 7.2–7.8 and total alkalinity 80–140 ppm. It is labeled compatible with chlorine, bromine and biguanide systems and made in the USA.

## What owners praise

- Easier and safer to store than liquid acid
- Brings down both pH and high alkalinity, which many tubs need after a fresh fill
- A 1.5-lb container lasts a small tub a long time
- Works with any sanitizer

## What owners complain about

- Easy to overshoot on small inflatable volumes if you dose without measuring
- Clumping after the container has been opened repeatedly in humid weather

## Who should buy it

If your water tests high on pH, which is common because jet aeration pushes it upward, this is the cleanest fit. If pH runs low, you want the [SpaChoice pH Increaser](/products/spa-choice-ph-increaser) instead, and most owners eventually keep both.
