If you own a Bestway Lay-Z-Spa or Coleman SaluSpa, the Type VI cartridge is the filter you will buy most often, and the most common mistake is ordering an Intex S1 by accident.

## What the manufacturer says

Bestway lists the Type VI as part 58323, at about 4.2" diameter by 3.1" high, for Lay-Z-Spa and Coleman SaluSpa inflatable hot tubs. Bestway's listings note the Lay-Z-Spa pump takes two cartridges at a time.

## What owners praise

- Exact fit in the Bestway/Coleman filter housing
- Quick swap with no tools
- Having extras makes rotation easy
- Clear water when changed on schedule

## What owners complain about

- Small size means frequent changes in busy tubs
- Some owners find generic VI cartridges fit loosely and prefer the branded part

## Who should buy it

If you own a Bestway or Coleman inflatable, this is the cleanest fit. If you own an Intex PureSpa, you need the [Intex Type S1](/products/intex-type-s1-filter-cartridge) instead. A [Scumbug](/products/scumbug-scum-absorber-2-pack) floating in the tub helps these small cartridges last longer by catching oils first.
