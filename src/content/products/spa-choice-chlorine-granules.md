For inflatable-spa owners who have chosen chlorine, sodium dichlor granules are the standard tool, and SpaChoice's 2-lb jar is a widely stocked way to buy them.

## What the manufacturer says

The listing specifies 99% sodium dichlor, a dose of 3 teaspoons per 500 gallons as needed, and about 1.4 ppm available chlorine per level teaspoon in 500 gallons. SpaChoice states it is made in the USA.

## What owners praise

- Granules dissolve quickly, so post-soak dosing is simple
- Teaspoon measuring makes it easy to fine-tune for small tubs
- One product can cover routine sanitizing and a heavier chlorine shock
- A jar size that lasts a typical inflatable tub a good while

## What owners complain about

- Readings drop quickly after heavy use, so dosing is more frequent than with a bromine floater
- Stabilizer buildup over months, which is inherent to dichlor rather than this brand

## Who should buy it

If your priority is control and quick response, this is the cleanest fit. If you want a once-a-week routine, a [bromine tablet setup](/products/spa-choice-bromine-tablets) needs less daily attention.
