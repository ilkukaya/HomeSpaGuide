The Greywood Deluxe is for buyers who want a 4-person inflatable that arrives with the comfort extras already in the box.

## Headline specs

Per Intex's published spec, the 28439E is a 77-inch round tub, 28 inches tall, with a 57-inch inner diameter and roughly 210 gallons of water at the fill line. It seats up to four adults, runs 140 floor bubble jets, and heats to a maximum of 104°F from a standard 110–120V outlet. Construction is Intex's Fiber-Tech system — internal fibers that tie the inner and outer walls together — plus a built-in hard water treatment system, a multi-color LED light and two foam headrests.

## What owners praise

- **Complete package.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently mention that nothing else needs to be bought to get started beyond chemicals.
- **Wall stiffness.** Fiber-Tech is repeatedly credited with keeping the sidewalls upright enough to lean against.
- **Easy setup.** The pump inflates the tub, so most owners report being filled within an hour.
- **Night-time ambience.** The LED light is a small detail that shows up often in positive reviews.

## What owners complain about

- **Slow heat-up.** A category-wide trait of 110V inflatables, but it catches first-time buyers off guard.
- **Bubbles, not massage.** Buyers expecting hard-shell style jets are disappointed.
- **Cold-weather heat loss.** Owner reports describe the heater struggling to hold temperature in freezing weather.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

If your priority is a comfortable, well-equipped tub for two to four people in spring through fall, this is the cleanest fit in Intex's range. If you don't care about the headrests, LED light or wood-grain look, the [Intex PureSpa Bubble](/products/intex-purespa-bubble) delivers the same footprint for less.

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, the Greywood has 140 jets versus 120 on both the PureSpa Bubble and the [Coleman SaluSpa Tahiti](/products/coleman-saluspa-tahiti-airjet), and it is taller and wider than the 71" x 26" Tahiti. The Tahiti's EnergySense cover and Freeze Shield give it the edge for cooler climates.
