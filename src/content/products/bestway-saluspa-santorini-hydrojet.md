The Santorini HydroJet is for buyers who want an inflatable that delivers actual jet massage, and who have the space and patience for a big tub.

## Headline specs

Per Bestway's published spec, the Santorini measures 85" x 85" x 31.5", holds about 314 gallons at 80% fill and seats 5–7 people. It has two massage systems: 180 AirJets in the floor and 10 HydroJet nozzles around the sidewalls. The heat range is 40–104°F on standard 110–120V power with a 3-prong plug. The box includes the pump (which inflates, heats, filters and runs the massage systems), cover, chemical floater, two filter cartridges and a repair patch.

## What owners praise

- **HydroJets.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently single out the water jets as the reason they chose this tub over bubble-only models.
- **Depth.** The 31.5-inch walls let taller bathers submerge their shoulders.
- **Space.** Multiple adults can stretch out without knocking knees.

## What owners complain about

- **Heat-up time** — the largest tub here, on the same 110V power as small tubs.
- **Weight and storage.** Draining and folding it is a two-person job.
- **Running cost** in cool weather, a category-wide trait that grows with water volume.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

If your priority is massage, this is the cleanest fit in the inflatable category because the HydroJets do something AirJets can't. If you mostly want a warm bubbly soak for two, a 4-person tub like the [Coleman SaluSpa Tahiti](/products/coleman-saluspa-tahiti-airjet) will heat faster and cost less to run.

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, the [Intex PureSpa Plus 6-Person](/products/intex-purespa-plus-6-person) (85" x 28", 170 bubble jets) matches its width but has no water jets. The [Bestway SaluSpa Hawaii 6–8 Person](/products/bestway-saluspa-hawaii-8-person) is wider at 93 inches with 190 AirJets, but is bubble-only.
