You cannot balance what you do not measure, and AquaChek's SPA 6-in-1 strips are the quickest way for a hot tub owner to know which chemical to reach for.

## What the manufacturer says

AquaChek lists six readings per strip: total bromine, total chlorine, free chlorine, pH, total alkalinity and total hardness. The product is spa-specific (model 552244), holds 50 strips, and uses a color chart on the bottle.

## What owners praise

- One dip covers sanitizer and balance in about the time it takes to lift the cover
- Works whether the tub runs bromine or chlorine
- A hardness reading, useful in hard-water areas
- Bottle chart makes the colors easy to compare in daylight

## What owners complain about

- Adjacent colors can be hard to tell apart, especially in the middle of a range
- Strips go bad if moisture gets into the bottle

## Who should buy it

If you are new to hot tubs, we selected this as the first testing tool to buy, because it tells you which of your other chemicals you actually need. If you want lab-style precision for troubleshooting, a liquid drop kit is the step up.
