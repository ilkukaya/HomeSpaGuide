When the original cover on a 4-person round PureSpa tears, fades or stops holding heat, the Intex 28523E is the factory replacement to buy.

## What the manufacturer says

Intex lists the 28523E for 4-person round PureSpa models, with dimensions of 78" x 28⅜". It uses high-density, thermal-resistant foam to reduce heat loss and closes with a belt and locking safety buckle. Intex positions it as an energy-efficient cover that also shields the tub from punctures, debris and weather.

## What owners praise

- Drop-in fit on the round 4-person PureSpa
- Faster heat-up and steadier temperatures when the tub stays covered
- The locking buckle keeps it seated in wind
- Easy for one person to put on and remove

## What owners complain about

- It is a soft cover, so rain can pool on top and it will not hold weight
- UV exposure fades and stiffens the PVC over several seasons, a common trait in inflatable covers

## Who should buy it

If you own a 4-person round PureSpa and your cover is damaged, this is the cleanest fit. If you own a 6-person or square PureSpa, or a Bestway or Coleman tub, look for the cover that matches your model number.
