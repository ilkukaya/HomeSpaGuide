If you want a sanitizer you can set and mostly forget for a week at a time, bromine tablets in a floater are the lowest-effort route, and SpaChoice's 1.5-lb jar is a common way to buy them.

## What the manufacturer says

Per the listing, these are 98% BCDMH tablets with a label dose of 3 tablets per 300 gallons every 5–7 days. They are designed for floating dispensers and in-line brominators, and SpaChoice notes they are made in the USA.

## What owners praise

- Steady, slow dissolution that keeps a floater going for days
- Noticeably less "pool smell" than chlorine, a theme across many owner reviews
- Easy to pair with most floaters sized for 1" tabs
- A resealable jar that stores well when kept dry

## What owners complain about

- On a fresh fill, tablets alone take a while to build a bromine reading; many owners add a sodium bromide starter or shock first
- Bromine readings can drift high if the floater is left wide open in a lightly used tub

## Who should buy it

If your priority is a low-touch weekly routine, this is the cleanest fit. If you prefer quick, precise dosing after each soak, chlorine granules suit that style better.
