If you own an Intex PureSpa, the Type S1 cartridge is the one consumable you will buy more than anything else, and this 6-pack of the genuine 29001E part is the simplest way to stay stocked.

## What the manufacturer says

Intex specifies the Type S1 (29001E) for its PureSpa inflatable hot tubs. The element is pleated Dacron paper. Intex's guidance is to clean the cartridge regularly and replace it about monthly, or sooner under heavy use.

## What owners praise

- Exact fit in the PureSpa filter housing, with no bypass around the seal
- Having spares on hand makes rotation easy
- Replacement takes seconds
- Consistent quality from pack to pack, per many reviews

## What owners complain about

- Paper cartridges are a recurring cost
- Some owners report clogging faster in heavily used or oily water, which is why a scum absorber helps

## Who should buy it

Every PureSpa owner should keep these in stock. If you own a Bestway or Coleman inflatable, you need the [Bestway Type VI cartridge](/products/bestway-type-vi-filter-cartridge) instead.
