The SaluSpa Milan is for families who want a mid-size round tub with cold-weather safeguards, without paying HydroJet prices.

## Headline specs

Per Bestway's published spec, the Milan measures 77" x 77" x 28", holds about 242 gallons at 80% fill and seats 4–6 people. It runs 140 AirJets, heats across a 40–104°F range on standard 110–120V power, and ships with an EnergySense insulated cover that Bestway claims is up to 40% more energy efficient than similar inflatable spas. Freeze Shield automatically heats the water to protect internal parts.

## What owners praise

- **Balanced size.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently describe it as roomy for four without the heating penalty of a 7-person tub.
- **Freeze Shield.** Frequently cited as the reason buyers chose it over similar-size tubs.
- **Easy setup.** The pump inflates the tub; most owners report soaking the next day.

## What owners complain about

- **Heat-up time** from a cold fill.
- **Bubble-only massage** for buyers expecting jets.
- **Cover wear** after a couple of seasons in full sun.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

Most buyers in this price range will find the Milan covers everything they need for family soaking from spring to fall, with some protection in cold snaps. If you want targeted massage, the [Bestway SaluSpa Santorini HydroJet](/products/bestway-saluspa-santorini-hydrojet) is the step up. If your space suits a square footprint, the [Bestway SaluSpa Hawaii](/products/bestway-hawaii-airjet) offers the same 140 jets.

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, the [Intex Greywood Deluxe](/products/intex-purespa-greywood) matches the Milan on size (77" x 28") and jet count (140) and adds headrests and an LED light, while the Milan adds Freeze Shield and the EnergySense cover.
