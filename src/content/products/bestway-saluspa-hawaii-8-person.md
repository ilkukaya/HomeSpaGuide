The 6–8 person Hawaii is for large households and hosts who want party-size seating without moving to a hard-shell tub.

## Headline specs

Per Bestway's published spec, this round Hawaii measures 93" x 93" x 28", holds about 380 gallons and seats 6–8 people with 190 AirJets. It heats to 104°F on standard 110–120V power. Bestway lists EnergySense insulated walls and cover (claimed up to 40% more energy efficient than similar inflatables), MaxHold fabric internal beams and Freeze Shield automatic anti-freeze heating. The box includes the pump, cover, chemical floater and a filter cartridge.

## What owners praise

- **Space.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently mention that groups can actually stretch out.
- **Value per seat.** Few ways exist to seat six-plus adults for inflatable money.
- **Cold-weather features.** Insulated walls and Freeze Shield are regularly cited.

## What owners complain about

- **Heat-up time** — the largest fill of any tub we cover, on 110V.
- **Electricity use** in cool weather rises with volume.
- **Footprint.** Some buyers underestimate how much level space 93 inches needs.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

If your priority is seating the most people, this is the cleanest fit in the inflatable category. If it will usually be two or three people, the extra water is a running-cost penalty — a 77-inch tub like the [Bestway SaluSpa Milan](/products/bestway-saluspa-milan-airjet) is the better buy.

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, the [Bestway SaluSpa Santorini HydroJet](/products/bestway-saluspa-santorini-hydrojet) is smaller (85" x 31.5", 5–7 people) but adds 10 HydroJets for real massage. The [Intex PureSpa Plus 6-Person](/products/intex-purespa-plus-6-person) (85", 170 jets) holds about 290 gallons, so it heats faster.
