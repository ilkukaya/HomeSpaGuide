A dirty cartridge restricts flow and makes every chemical work harder. Leisure Time Filter Clean is a widely available way to deep-clean spa filters instead of replacing them early.

## What the manufacturer says

Leisure Time lists Filter Clean as a cartridge cleaner that removes dirt, oil, grease and scale to improve flow and filtration. The recommended method is an overnight soak about every three months. It is labeled compatible with chlorine, bromine, ozone and mineral systems.

## What owners praise

- Cartridges come out visibly cleaner than after a hose rinse
- Better flow and clearer water after reinstalling
- One bottle covers several soaks
- Makes hard-shell cartridges last noticeably longer, per many owner reviews

## What owners complain about

- The overnight soak means planning ahead, or keeping a spare cartridge
- Needs a thorough rinse or foam can follow

## Who should buy it

If you own a hard-shell spa with cartridges like the [Pleatco PRB50-IN](/products/pleatco-prb50-in-spa-filter), this is the cleanest fit, because extending cartridge life saves real money. Inflatable owners with inexpensive paper cartridges benefit less, but it still helps if you rotate and reuse them.
