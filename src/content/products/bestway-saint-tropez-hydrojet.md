If you want a tub that's actually rated for winter operation, the Saint Tropez is one of the few inflatables that fits. Bestway's freeze-shield mode keeps the pump cycling at low power any time the water temp drops near freezing — preventing the catastrophic pump damage that kills off-season inflatable spas.

The HydroJet bank is genuinely powerful, though you'll want to flip back to AirJet mode for daily soaks (it draws less power and runs much quieter).
