A sanitizer keeps water safe, but a weekly oxidizer is what keeps it clear and fresh-smelling, and Leisure Time Renew is one of the most widely stocked non-chlorine shocks for hot tubs.

## What the manufacturer says

Leisure Time describes Renew as a fast-dissolving, pH-buffered non-chlorine shock that removes oils and residue causing odor and irritation. It is labeled for chlorine, bromine and mineral systems. The routine: add with filtration running, leave the cover off for 15 minutes, repeat weekly.

## What owners praise

- Clearer, fresher water within a day of the weekly dose
- No lingering chlorine smell
- Quick return to soaking
- A 2.2-lb container lasts a typical home tub a long time

## What owners complain about

- Owners new to MPS sometimes expect it to sanitize on its own
- Some test strips show a temporarily inflated total chlorine reading after dosing

## Who should buy it

If you run bromine, we selected this because it regenerates bromide and handles the oxidizing job tablets cannot. Chlorine users can shock with a larger dose of [dichlor granules](/products/spa-choice-chlorine-granules) instead, though MPS avoids the extra stabilizer.
