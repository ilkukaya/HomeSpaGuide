For hard-shell hot tubs that take the 50 sq ft drop-in size, the Pleatco PRB50-IN is a well-known name-brand replacement with clear cross-reference numbers.

## What the manufacturer says

Pleatco lists the PRB50-IN at 5" diameter by about 13-5/16" long with 50 sq ft of filter area. It cross-references to Unicel C-4950 and Filbur FC-2390. Pleatco describes point-bonded fabric, reinforced center cores and break-resistant end caps.

## What owners praise

- Correct fit when the part number matches
- Sturdy cores and end caps that survive repeated cleaning
- Noticeably clearer water after swapping out a worn cartridge
- A drop-in design with no tools needed

## What owners complain about

- Buying by tub brand instead of by part number leads to wrong-size orders
- Price is higher than generic "compatible" cartridges

## Who should buy it

If your existing cartridge reads PRB50-IN, C-4950 or FC-2390, this is the cleanest fit. If you own an inflatable, this is not your filter; Intex PureSpa owners need the [Type S1](/products/intex-type-s1-filter-cartridge) and Bestway or Coleman owners need the [Type VI](/products/bestway-type-vi-filter-cartridge).
