The SaluSpa Atlantis is for families who want a square, corner-friendly tub with Coleman's styling and cold-weather features included.

## Headline specs

Per the manufacturer's published spec, the Atlantis measures 71" x 71" x 28", holds about 222 gallons at 80% fill and seats 4–6 people with 140 AirJets. It heats to 104°F on standard 110–120V power. The EnergySense cover is claimed to be up to 40% more energy efficient than similar inflatables, the DuraPlus 3-layer walls are positioned as more puncture- and stretch-resistant than standard PVC, and Freeze Shield automatically heats the water to protect internal parts.

## What owners praise

- **Square shape.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently mention how well it fits deck corners.
- **Defined seating.** Corners give each person a spot to lean into.
- **Setup.** The pump inflates the tub and most owners are soaking within a day.

## What owners complain about

- **Crowding** with more than four adults.
- **Heat-up time** from a cold fill on 110V.
- **Cover wear** after extended sun exposure.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

If your priority is a square footprint with freeze protection, this is a clean fit — and so is the [Bestway SaluSpa Hawaii](/products/bestway-hawaii-airjet), so compare prices. Couples should look at the smaller [Coleman SaluSpa Tahiti](/products/coleman-saluspa-tahiti-airjet).

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, the round [Bestway SaluSpa Milan](/products/bestway-saluspa-milan-airjet) has the same 140 jets and 28-inch height but a larger 77-inch diameter and about 242 gallons at 80% fill versus the Atlantis's 222.
