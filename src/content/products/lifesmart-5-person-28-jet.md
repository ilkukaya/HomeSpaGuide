The Lifesmart 5-Person 28-Jet is for households that want a hard-shell hot tub with a lounger but can't, or don't want to, add 240V wiring.

## Headline specs

Per the manufacturer's published spec, this Lifesmart tub measures 72" x 81" x 34", holds about 300 gallons and weighs roughly 420 lbs dry. It seats five, with one full-body lounger plus bench seating, and runs 28 water jets from a 1.0 HP pump on standard 110V power. The shell is rotationally molded polyethylene with full foam insulation, and it includes an ozone water care system, an illuminated waterfall, multi-color LED lighting and a thermal locking cover.

## What owners praise

- **The lounger.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently highlight the lounger as the reason they chose this tub over the 4-person model.
- **Installation.** Plugging in rather than hiring an electrician is a recurring theme.
- **Features for the price.** Ozone, waterfall and LED are all standard.

## What owners complain about

- **Heat-up time** for 300 gallons on 110V.
- **Jet pressure** is gentler than 240V acrylic spas.
- **Delivery and placement** — at over 400 lbs dry it needs several people to move.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

If your priority is hard-shell comfort and a lounger without new wiring, this is the cleanest fit because nothing else we cover offers both. If you mainly soak as a couple, the smaller [Lifesmart LS100](/products/lifesmart-ls100-4-person) heats faster and costs much less. If a multi-season lifespan isn't a priority, the [Bestway SaluSpa Santorini HydroJet](/products/bestway-saluspa-santorini-hydrojet) adds water jets to an inflatable at a fraction of the price.

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, it offers 28 jets and 300 gallons versus 13 jets and 200 gallons on the LS100, and 34-inch depth versus 31.5 inches on the Santorini.
