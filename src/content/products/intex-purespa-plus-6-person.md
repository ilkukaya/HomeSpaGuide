The PureSpa Plus 6-Person is for families who like the Intex platform but need more room than the 77-inch tubs provide.

## Headline specs

Per Intex's published spec for the 28431EP: 85-inch outer diameter, 65-inch inner diameter, 28 inches tall, about 290 gallons of water and seating for up to six. It runs 170 bubble jets — the most in Intex's bubble-massage lineup — and heats to 104°F on a standard 110–120V outlet. The set includes built-in hard water treatment, an energy-efficient cover, two headrests and an LED light.

## What owners praise

- **Room.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently note that four adults can sit without tangling legs.
- **Jet coverage.** 170 jets give a lively bubble field around the whole perimeter.
- **Complete kit.** Headrests, cover and light are included, so there's little extra to buy.
- **Stable walls.** Fiber-Tech construction is regularly praised for holding shape.

## What owners complain about

- **Heat-up time** — more water on the same 110V power as smaller tubs.
- **Cold-weather performance.** Owner reports describe the heater struggling to hold 104°F in freezing temperatures.
- **Storage bulk** when draining for winter.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

If your priority is space and bubble coverage for a family, this is the cleanest fit in Intex's range. If you want directional water-jet massage, look at the [Bestway SaluSpa Santorini HydroJet](/products/bestway-saluspa-santorini-hydrojet). If only two people will use it most of the time, the smaller [Intex Greywood Deluxe](/products/intex-purespa-greywood) will heat faster and cost less to run.

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, it is wider than the 77-inch [Bestway SaluSpa Milan](/products/bestway-saluspa-milan-airjet) and has 30 more jets (170 vs. 140), but the Milan lists Freeze Shield and an EnergySense cover.
