If you run bromine tablets without a built-in brominator, you need a floater, and U.S. Pool Supply's dispenser stands out for its fine flow adjustment.

## What the manufacturer says

The listing describes a heavy-duty, weighted floating dispenser that holds 1" chlorine or bromine tablets, with 13 adjustable flow-control settings. It is sold for spas, hot tubs and small pools.

## What owners praise

- More flow settings than basic floaters, which helps in small tubs
- Floats upright rather than tipping over
- Easy twist-open refills
- Keeps tablets away from the liner or shell

## What owners complain about

- Drifting into the filter intake or corners
- Plastic can fade or crack after long sun exposure, a common floater trait

## Who should buy it

If your priority is dialing in a steady bromine level in an inflatable or small hard-shell tub, this is the cleanest fit. Pair it with [SpaChoice bromine tablets](/products/spa-choice-bromine-tablets) and confirm readings with [test strips](/products/aquachek-spa-6-in-1-test-strips).
