The PureSpa Bubble Massage is for first-time buyers who want the Intex 4-person platform at the lowest entry price.

## Headline specs

Intex's published spec for the 28425EP: 77-inch outer diameter, 57-inch inner diameter, 28 inches tall, about 210 gallons of water, seating for up to four adults and 120 bubble jets. It heats to 104°F on a standard 110–120V outlet, includes a built-in hard water treatment system and ships with an insulated cover, filter pump and inflation hose. The finish is a plain Sahara Tan.

## What owners praise

- **Price-to-size ratio.** Owner reviews on Amazon consistently call it one of the cheapest ways to get a genuinely usable four-person tub.
- **Simple setup.** The pump handles inflation; most buyers report being filled the same afternoon.
- **Parts availability.** Filters, covers and replacement parts are shared across the PureSpa line and easy to find.

## What owners complain about

- **Heating time and heat loss in cold weather** — the common 110V inflatable complaint.
- **Bubbles, not massage.** Buyers expecting strong jets are disappointed.
- **Cover durability** — several owners report replacing the cover after a couple of seasons.

## Who should buy it — and who shouldn't

Most buyers in this price range will find it does exactly what they need: a warm, bubbly soak for two to four people. If you want headrests and lighting included, step up to the [Intex Greywood Deluxe](/products/intex-purespa-greywood). If you want a tub better set up for cooler evenings, the [Coleman SaluSpa Tahiti](/products/coleman-saluspa-tahiti-airjet) adds an EnergySense cover and Freeze Shield, at the cost of a slightly smaller 71-inch footprint.

## How it compares

Based on the spec sheets, it matches the Tahiti on jet count (120 each) and edges it on size (77" x 28" vs. 71" x 26"), while the Greywood adds 20 jets and comfort extras.
